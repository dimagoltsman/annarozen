Appointment policy

Booking guidelines, cancellations and deposits

To ensure a seamless and elevated experience for every client, we kindly ask that you review our booking guidelines.

I completely understand that plans can change and things come up. Life happens. I truly respect your time, and as a small business owner, I also balance my own schedule, family, and commitments.

Booking and cancellations

  • Appointments may be rescheduled or canceled with at least 12 hours' notice.
  • Changes made within 12 hours may incur a 50% service fee.
  • Missed appointments may be charged at the full service amount.
  • All appointments must be confirmed 12 hours prior to your visit.
  • If your appointment is not confirmed, I may reach out to you before releasing the reserved time.
  • Late cancellation and no-show fees will be requested separately, as no payment information is kept on file.
  • Any outstanding cancellation or no-show fees must be settled before a new appointment can be booked.

Arrival

  • Please arrive on time for your appointment.
  • A 10-minute grace period is extended.
  • Arriving later may result in a shortened or rescheduled appointment to respect other clients' time.

Guests and children

  • For the comfort and safety of all, we kindly recommend not bringing children.
  • This helps avoid distractions and ensures the best results.
  • Please note that there are professional tools and equipment in the studio that may not be safe for children.

Deposits

  • Following a late cancellation or missed appointment, a 50% deposit will be required to secure future bookings.
  • The deposit will be applied toward your service.
  • Deposits are non-refundable, but may be transferred if rescheduled with at least 12 hours' notice.

Experience

  • We reserve time exclusively for each client, ensuring a private and personalized experience.
  • Clients with repeated last-minute cancellations may not be able to be rebooked.

We truly appreciate your understanding and respect for our time and valued clientele. Thank you for choosing us. We look forward to welcoming you.
